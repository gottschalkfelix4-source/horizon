Neo Vitae is the NeoForge continuation of Blood Magic (originally by WayofTime and the Blood Magic
contributors) and is released under the MIT licence (author names and repository link to be
confirmed against the jar's `META-INF/neoforge.mods.toml`). This port contains no code from it,
nor from Blood Magic: it is written from the mod's observed behaviour and public description,
following Horizon's porting rules (docs/modpacks.md, "Licences").
